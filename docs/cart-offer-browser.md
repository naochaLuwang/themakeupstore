# Cart offer cards and eligible products

The cart uses a compact 64px `CartOfferCards` strip in both layouts: **Buy 2 Get 1 Free**, a short brand/category/selected-product label, **Add N more** (or **N free applied**) and **Shop →**. Long selections truncate and show their full scope in the info popover. There are no gift illustrations, progress bars, or terms paragraphs in the strip. Product-specific campaigns always say **selected** products so they do not imply whole-brand eligibility.

Applied rewards stay visible after qualification. The panel footer shows how many more items unlock the first reward; after qualification it shows applied free items and a short next-reward hint when a further set is partly complete. Units already allocated to another campaign cannot advance this campaign's progress. Financial allocation and checkout calculations are unchanged.

**Shop** opens an accessible Radix dialog: a bottom sheet on phones and a 480px panel on desktop. It has a short header, search alongside a filter icon, horizontal brand chips, and a two-column product grid with 20-product pages. Categories stay behind **Filters** until requested. Cards show short product names, prices and **+ Add** or **Choose shade**. In-stock products come first; sold-out and fully consumed stock are disabled. A slim footer shows progress and **View bag (N) →**.

**Choose shade** replaces the grid with a dedicated view inside the same dialog, with a product photo, exact variant prices, labelled radio tiles, and one sticky **Add to bag** button. Real distinct variant images appear in the tiles; variants without them use named options rather than invented colours. No variant is selected automatically. Sold-out shades are disabled. Going back restores the filter, page, grid scroll position and product-button focus; successful adds also return to the grid. Cart changes and background product refreshes update stock limits in both views. Adding uses the normal cart store, with brand, category and regular-price metadata for synchronous promotion previews. Closing preserves the cart and restores focus to the original Shop button.

Replacement campaigns show **Original prices apply ⓘ** once in the header, original variant prices on cards, and discounted normal prices as **Usually**. Detailed terms and the complete campaign selection are in a keyboard-accessible info popover. Combination campaigns show current selling prices and **Product discounts apply**. Flash-sale eligibility and variant exclusions use the existing shared pricing helper.

## Data and performance

- `GET /api/offers/[id]/products` authenticates its own session once and supports guests. Responses are private/no-store. Active dates, usage limits, supported rule type and session-owned once-per-user usage are checked server-side. Client-supplied owner IDs have no effect.
- The server reads exact campaign targets, both primary and junction categories, active products, and `product_variants.stock`. Target/catalog reads are chunked and paginated to avoid database row limits. Matching catalogs cache for 30 seconds under promotion/flash invalidation tags; user eligibility is outside the shared catalog cache. Authoritative quotes and payment still fetch current stock, prices and rules.
- The first relevant campaign's first product page prefetches after 800ms without blocking cart pricing. Hover/focus/touch warms the selected panel and page. First-page requests deduplicate, expire within 10 seconds or at the next offer/flash boundary, and use separate customer keys. Only 12 first-page entries are retained.
- Search waits 250ms; changing filters aborts the previous request and ignores late responses. Requests time out after 10 seconds. Open panels refresh within 30 seconds or at the next pricing boundary. Account changes remount the panel and clear its displayed results and selection.

## Deployment and verification

This feature introduces no database migration or native changes. Deploy with the existing finance, promotion, BXGY pricing-mode and flash-sale migrations described in `docs/flash-sales.md`; those earlier requirements still apply. No production data was modified during this work.

Coverage includes exact eligibility, secondary categories, more than 1,000 targets/products, readable labels, repeating/overlapping progress, flash exclusions/boundaries, guest/private responses, session-owned usage checks, stock limits (including unchanged quantities), explicit shade selection, variant prices/photos, back navigation, deferred filters, stale responses, retry paths and cache expiry. Browser checks with fixture data and existing local sample images at 320px, 390px and 1440px verify the 64px strip, two-column grid, no horizontal overflow, keyboard shade selection, instant reward updates, Escape dismissal and opener focus restoration. Sample images in the browser fixture are illustrative; production uses existing catalog images. A real customer checkout remains part of deployment smoke testing.
